How to use this tool
1. What the tool assesses: It reviews your organization across 4 strategic pillars and 12 distinct functional dimensions to benchmark internal infrastructure, operational capacity, growth mechanics, and technical governance.
2. How to select checkboxes: Mark a checkbox if that item is broadly established and lived in day-to-day work; it does not have to be perfect in every corner. Leave it unchecked if it only happens occasionally, depends on individual people, or has not really started yet.
3. Why each item matters: Every item has a ? icon next to it. It explains what the item looks like in practice and why it matters; worth a look even where you already know your answer.
4. How the score is calculated: Each dimension gets a maturity score from 0.0 to 5.0 based on what you've established. Foundational items count for more than secondary ones, so your overall score reflects what actually matters most.
5. Focus areas interpretation: Your top focus areas aren't just your lowest scores. We combine how big each gap is with how much that area drives your growth, so you fix the bottleneck with the most leverage first, not just the lowest number.

Understanding Your Bottlenecks

Having worked with more than 40 clients since 2019, we see the same two problems in growing companies: they lose sight of what customers actually pay them for, and everything takes longer than it used to. Teams end up with too many tools, work that does not connect, and nobody clearly responsible. From then on, the days go into reacting.

We work by one rule: "Fall in love with the problem, not the solution". Instead of reaching for the quick fix, another licence or another hire, we find out what is actually causing the friction. That is usually cheaper than the quick fix, and it lets your team spend its time on the product itself.
