How to use this tool
1. What the tool assesses: It reviews your organization across 4 strategic pillars and 12 distinct functional dimensions to benchmark internal infrastructure, operational capacity, growth mechanics, and technical governance.
2. How to select checkboxes: Mark a checkbox only if that item is completely and firmly established as a uniform standard across your organization. Leave it unchecked if it is practiced inconsistently, ad-hoc, or is still a work-in-progress.
3. How the score is calculated: Each dimension gets a maturity score from 0.0 to 5.0 based on what you've established. Foundational items count for more than secondary ones, so your overall score reflects what actually matters most.
4. Focus areas interpretation: Your top focus areas aren't just your lowest scores. We combine how big each gap is with how much that area drives your growth — so you fix the bottleneck with the most leverage first, not just the lowest number.

💡 Understanding Your Bottlenecks

Having worked with more than 40 clients since 2019, we find that most scaling businesses struggle with two main things: losing focus on their value chain and watching their development speed slow down as the company grows. Teams end up drowning in tool sprawl, disconnected tasks, and fragmented responsibilities. When that happens, the company enters survival mode.
